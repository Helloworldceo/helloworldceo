## About

Software / AI Engineer shipping full products end-to-end, across applied AI (RAG, agentic workflows, NL-to-SQL), AI/LLM security (prompt-injection red-teaming) and network security (traffic analysis, DPI-resistant tunneling). I also do full-stack and solution engineering. I'm based in Suzhou, China, on a CSC Scholarship at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and I have client-facing delivery experience with international customers on EV charging, energy storage and microgrid projects.

- 🔭 Currently building **[AI-Agent-Red-Team](https://github.com/Helloworldceo/AI-Agent-Red-Team)**: adversarial testing for tool-calling and RAG-backed agents, not just raw prompts, with a daily research bot that grows its own findings log
- 🌱 MSc Software Engineering at USTC, with a thesis on multi-agent AI decision support for PV-BESS feasibility
- 🗣️ English (fluent) · 中文 (HSK4) · Français (B1) · Amharic & Afaan Oromoo (native)
